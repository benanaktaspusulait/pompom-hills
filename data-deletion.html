User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Users and account holders may request deletion of information associated with Pompom Hills Creative Intelligence.

How to Request Deletion

Pompom Hills Creative Intelligence is an internal read-only analytics application used for content published on Pompom Hills' own Facebook and Instagram accounts. If you believe information associated with you or an account you manage is held by the application, follow the steps below.

  1. 1

    Send an email

    Email admin@pompomhills.com.

  2. 2

    Use the request subject

    Pompom Hills Data Deletion Request

  3. 3

    Provide identifying information

    Include your name, the relevant Facebook Page or Instagram Professional Account name, and enough information for us to identify the relevant connection or stored data.

    Do not send passwords, access tokens or other login credentials.

Review and Processing

Valid requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable timeframe. We may contact you to confirm the scope of the request or to verify that you are authorised to make it. Verification requests will never ask for a password or access token.

Information That May Be Deleted

Depending on the data associated with the request, deletion may include:

  • stored analytics snapshots;
  • stored media metadata;
  • Facebook Page or Instagram Professional Account identifiers associated with the integration; and
  • stored access credentials, where applicable.

Limited Retention

Some limited information may be retained where reasonably required for security, legal compliance or operational record-keeping. Any retained information will remain limited to the applicable purpose.

More Information

For more detail about how the application handles information, read the Pompom Hills Privacy Policy.
