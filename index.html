Privacy & Data Information

Pompom Hills Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Pompom Hills Creative Intelligence is an internal analytics application used by Pompom Hills to retrieve read-only performance insights for content published on Pompom Hills' own Facebook and Instagram accounts.

1. About This Application

Pompom Hills Creative Intelligence is operated by Pompom Hills as an internal business analytics tool. It helps authorised Pompom Hills personnel understand the performance of content published on the Pompom Hills Facebook Page and Instagram Professional Account.

The application is not a public software-as-a-service product and is not offered for third parties to connect or manage their social media accounts.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted through Meta, the application may access information associated with accounts owned or managed by Pompom Hills, including:

  • Facebook Page identifiers and account information;
  • Instagram Professional Account identifiers;
  • published media and Reel identifiers;
  • captions, timestamps, media type and permalinks;
  • views, plays, reach and impressions;
  • likes, comments, shares, saves and interaction totals; and
  • other content-performance metrics made available through applicable Meta APIs.

The application is designed for Pompom Hills' own managed business accounts. It does not invite members of the public to submit personal information.

3. How We Use Information

Information obtained through the read-only analytics integration is used solely for internal business purposes, including:

  • measuring content and Reel performance;
  • maintaining historical performance comparisons;
  • identifying viewing and engagement trends;
  • identifying content that performs well;
  • evaluating audience engagement at an aggregate content level;
  • improving future Pompom Hills content; and
  • creating internal analytics reports and creative-performance insights.

4. What We Do Not Do

Through this analytics integration, Pompom Hills does not:

  • sell or rent personal data;
  • share Meta data with unrelated third parties;
  • use Meta data to target individual users with advertising;
  • publish Facebook or Instagram content;
  • send messages on behalf of users;
  • manage comments; or
  • process private messages.

5. Data Storage and Retention

The application may store media metadata and analytics snapshots so that content performance can be compared over time. Pompom Hills limits stored information to what is reasonably necessary for internal analytics and reporting.

Analytics data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide historical performance analysis, unless deletion is requested or retention is no longer necessary.

6. Security

Access credentials are stored securely and access tokens are not intentionally exposed to the public. Access to the application and its stored analytics is limited to authorised Pompom Hills personnel and developers. Pompom Hills uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to this internal analytics use case.

7. Meta Platform

The application uses Meta APIs to retrieve authorised account and content-performance information. Use of Facebook and Instagram is also subject to the applicable Meta terms, policies and account settings. This policy describes how Pompom Hills handles information within Pompom Hills Creative Intelligence.

8. Children's Privacy

Although Pompom Hills produces family-oriented content, Pompom Hills Creative Intelligence is an internal business tool. It is not directed to children and does not intentionally collect personal information directly from children.

9. Data Deletion

Users or account holders may request deletion of data associated with the application. Instructions are available on the User Data Deletion page.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
